ABOUT AIR QUALITY MANAGEMENT

Air quality management in the Philippines is guided by national environmental laws, DENR administrative orders, EMB memorandum circulars, technical guidelines, and related policies. These instruments establish requirements for air quality monitoring, pollution control, permitting, standards, data handling, and implementation.

This is a concise reference. Consult the official EMB source for complete requirements, definitions, responsibilities, and current policy text.
